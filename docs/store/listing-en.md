## Description

⏰ Once a day, at a time nobody knows, your phone rings.
Everyone gets 2 minutes to start today's mini-game — the same game, the same level, for the whole planet. Only one attempt counts.

**24 mini-games, one per day**
Mini golf, penalties, basketball, bowling, pétanque, table tennis, air hockey, curling, archery, slalom, pool, darts, endless tower, fishing, parking, survival, flap, run, snake, karts, word flash, flash memory, mental math… Every game lasts 2 minutes at most and is played with one hand.

**The same challenge for everyone**
Same golf course, same wind, same goalkeeper: compare yourself with your friends, your country and the whole world.

**Keep your streak**
Play on time every day to grow your streak 🔥. One joker per month saves you from a missed day.

**With friends**
Add friends by nickname, link or QR code, see their results once you've played, react with an emoji and share your daily card.

No ads. No pay-to-win. Just one alarm, two minutes, one game.
