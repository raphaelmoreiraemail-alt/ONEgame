*Last updated: [PUBLICATION DATE]*

## Data we collect

| Data | Why | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| Anonymous account ID (Firebase Authentication) | Run your account without sign-up | Performance of the service |
| Nickname, avatar | Identify you to your friends and in leaderboards | Performance of the service |
| Region, language, country (from the phone settings) | Alarm time, region / country / language leaderboards | Performance of the service |
| Scores, statistics and the touch log of each official game | Leaderboards, streak, anti-cheat | Legitimate interest |
| Friends list, emoji reactions | Social features | Performance of the service |
| SHA-256 fingerprint of your phone number — **optional** | Let your contacts find you | Consent |
| Fingerprints of the numbers in your address book — **optional**, never in clear | Find friends who already play | Consent |
| Notification token | Send you the daily alarm | Performance of the service |
| Usage statistics (Firebase Analytics) and crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics) | Improve the app | Legitimate interest |

We do not sell any data, show no ads and do no advertising profiling.

## Processors

Data is hosted by Google Firebase (Google Ireland Ltd / Google LLC) under the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

## Retention

As long as your account exists. Touch logs are kept 12 months at most.

## Your rights

You can access, correct or delete your data. **Account deletion is available in the app** (Settings › Account › Delete my account): all your data is then permanently erased. For any other request: **[CONTACT ADDRESS]**.

## Children

The app is not intended for children under 13.
